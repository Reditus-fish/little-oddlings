# Art and asset provenance

The source code, dialogue, documentation, and code-drawn SVG characters, objects,
and habitats are offered under the repository's MIT license.

The two PNG concept sheets in `public/assets/` were generated with OpenAI image
generation. Both are preserved as visual development references: the earlier soft
clay treatment and the later Japanese-inspired cartoon treatment. They are not
hand-drawn production sprites. See `docs/art-generation.md` for prompts and history.
The project permits reuse of these sheets to the extent its contributors hold
rights in them; it does not claim exclusive rights in generated imagery.

The playable artwork is drawn in editable SVG code (`cartoon.js`, `soft.js`,
`objects.js`, and `index.html`). The application uses system fonts and no remotely
loaded graphics, audio, fonts, trackers, or paid art packages.

Contributed art must be yours to share under this license. Credit its author and
record any external asset's original source and license here before including it.
Do not submit recognizable third-party mascots or unlicensed references.

The Nap Committee concept animation (`public/promo/scene.js`) uses original
code-drawn vector artwork and an authored timeline, also under MIT. The MP4 and GIF
exports contain that artwork and no music or third-party footage. These are staged
concept illustrations, not recordings of autonomous gameplay.

The v0.4 desktop voice is original procedural audio (`public/pet/voice.js`),
shared under MIT. It contains no spoken language, samples, or speech synthesis.
The current concept-film export uses these cues and removes the earlier textual
captions and title. Prior concept art remains archived as development history.

The README banner and Easter egg still use the app's own SVG renderers. The
fortune image is a screenshot of the macOS build with a colored background.
